Privacy notice.

Short version: we keep your email to send you what you signed up for, and nothing else. Last updated 16 October 2026.

Who we are

Double Trouble Studios is an independent game studio from Finland. You can reach us at noah.doubletroublestudios@gmail.com. We are the controller of the data described below.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you join the crew.
  • Timestamps for when you signed up, confirmed your email and last signed in.
  • Sign-in tokens. One-time links for confirming and signing in, and a session token that keeps you signed in on your device.

We do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

Why we use it

To send you your sign-in links and the updates you asked for, such as devlogs and early access news, and to let you read the members-only devlog. Our legal basis is your consent, which you give by entering your email and confirming it. You can withdraw it at any time.

Who handles it for us

  • Supabase stores the database.
  • Make.com runs the automation that sends emails.
  • Google (Gmail) delivers the emails.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website.
  • Google Fonts serves the fonts. Your browser contacts Google to load them, which shares your IP address with Google.

These providers may process data outside the EU. We do not sell your data or share it with anyone else.

Your browser

We set no tracking cookies. When you sign in, your browser stores one item in local storage (dts_session) so the site recognises you. Signing out or clearing site data removes it.

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it. Every email we send has an unsubscribe link. Unsubscribing stops all email and signs you out everywhere.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, and you can withdraw your consent at any time. Email us and we will act on it. You also have the right to complain to the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman (tietosuojavaltuutettu) at tietosuoja.fi.
