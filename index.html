02 About the studio

Double Trouble Studios is a small independent team building games with Unity. We prototype fast, share our progress openly, and let our community shape what we make next.

03 How we work

Build in the open

Rough prototypes get shared early. You see what works, what breaks, and why we cut things.

Playtest first

Feedback from real players beats guessing. Early access members shape what ships next.

Small and sharp

A tiny crew means fast decisions, no committees, and every feature earns its place.

07 FAQ

What do I get by joining the crew?

Access to the devlog, release news and early access invites. Members are the first to hear about new builds.

Is it free?

Yes. Joining the crew is free.

Do I need a password?

No. Enter your email, click the link we send you, and you're signed in on that device.

How often will you email me?

Only when there's something worth sharing. Every email has an unsubscribe link.

What do you do with my email?

We use it to send your sign-in link and the updates you signed up for. Nothing else. Details are in the privacy notice.

How do I leave?

Use the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email. It takes one click.

How do I contact the studio?

Use the contact page. We read everything ourselves.
